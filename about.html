About Oguva

A digital marketing agency built around one thing: getting you enquiries.

Our story

Why Oguva exists

Oguva began with a simple observation: most businesses do not need more marketing noise, they need more enquiries. Owners were being sold impressions, likes and reports that looked busy but never rang the phone.

So we built an agency around outcomes instead. Every campaign we run is judged by one question — did it bring a real customer to your business? That is why our work is shaped around calls, forms and WhatsApp messages rather than charts that never turn into revenue.

The other thing we noticed is that search itself is changing. People no longer only type into Google; they ask ChatGPT, Gemini and AI answer boxes. If your business is not part of those answers, it becomes invisible to a growing share of buyers. Answer Engine Optimization is not a buzzword for us — it is how we keep our clients visible as search moves from links to answers.
